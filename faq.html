Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the ball jump around or fly to the top?

Pitch detectors can sometimes mistake a harmonic for the main note, which sounds like an octave jump. Hum a clear, steady “mmm” or “ooo” tone, avoid whispering, and recalibrate in a quiet room.

The ball does not move. What should I do?

Check that microphone permission is allowed for this site and that another app is not using the microphone. Check your device’s input level, then reload and try again. You can also choose touch mode as a fallback.

Does Hum Runner record my voice?

No. When you use the microphone, audio is analyzed live in your browser to estimate pitch; it is not recorded or uploaded by the game. See the Privacy Policy for details about analytics and local score storage.

Why does the ball fall when I stop humming?

When the game does not detect a clear pitch, gravity pulls the ball down. That is intentional—silence is one of the controls.

I cannot sing. Can I still play?

Yes. You do not need to sing a song or match a particular note. Calibration uses your own comfortable low and high notes, so a gentle hum is enough to try.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

It is designed for modern browsers, including Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android. Microphone play requires a secure site and browser permission. Touch mode is available if mic access is not suitable.

Why is it hard to play in a noisy place?

Background sound can interfere with pitch detection or be mistaken for your voice. Move somewhere quieter, hum steadily near the phone, and recalibrate.

Is the game free?

Yes, Hum Runner is free to play. The current version does not display ads; if advertising is added later to help cover costs, the Privacy Policy will be updated.
