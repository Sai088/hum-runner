How to Play

Guide the ball through the gaps by changing your hum’s pitch. If you prefer not to use a microphone, choose touch mode on the start screen.

The basics

  1. Tap Start and allow mic, then approve microphone access in your browser.
  2. Calibrate: hum your lowest comfortable note for three seconds, then your highest comfortable note for three seconds.
  3. Hum to steer: a higher pitch moves the ball up, and a lower pitch moves it down. Silence lets it sink.
  4. Pass through the gaps in the walls to score points. Hitting a wall ends the run.

Tips for calibrating your voice

  • Hold each note steadily for the full calibration prompt.
  • Do not strain to reach a high note; choose one that feels comfortable.
  • Keep the phone roughly 15–20 cm (6–8 in) from your mouth.
  • Calibrate in a quiet room so background sounds are less likely to confuse pitch detection.
  • If the ball responds badly, try calibrating again with a slightly wider range of comfortable notes.

Tips for a higher score

  • Glide gradually between notes rather than jumping abruptly.
  • Use silence to drop when you need to descend.
  • Look ahead to the next gap and start moving toward it early.
  • Warm up with a few gentle hums before starting; stop if your voice feels uncomfortable.
