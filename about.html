About Hum Runner

Hum Runner is a free browser game you play by humming. A higher pitch moves the ball up, a lower pitch moves it down, and when you stop humming, gravity makes it sink. There is nothing to download and no controller to find—just open the game and choose microphone or touch controls.

Why I made it

I wanted a fun weekend project that tried something different from the usual tap-and-swipe games. Controlling a game with a hum felt like a playful little experiment. It is especially funny when friends watch you concentrate on humming into your phone while trying to slip through a gap.

Who made it

Hum Runner was made by Teja, a software tester who likes finding how things break and fixing them. If you find a bug or have an idea, please get in touch.

How it works

With microphone access, the game analyzes the pitch of your voice live in your browser to steer the ball. Audio is not recorded or uploaded. Read more in the Privacy Policy.
