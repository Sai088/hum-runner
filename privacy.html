Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how Hum Runner at hum-runner.netlify.app handles information when you play the game or browse its pages.

Microphone

If you choose microphone play, your browser provides audio input so Hum Runner can estimate pitch and move the ball. This processing happens live in your browser. The game does not record, store, or transmit microphone audio. You can choose touch controls instead or deny microphone permission.

Information stored on your device

Your best score is saved in this browser’s local storage so it can be shown the next time you play. It is not sent to the game. You can clear it by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand site traffic, such as aggregate visit counts, general device and browser categories, countries, and referrers. We use these reports to understand overall usage, not to identify visitors by name. Cloudflare provides this service; see Cloudflare’s privacy policy for information about its processing.

Advertising and cookies

The current version of Hum Runner does not serve Google AdSense ads, so it does not set advertising cookies. If advertising is added in the future, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on visits to this and other sites. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Ads Settings. You can also control cookies in your browser. A consent message will be shown where required by law before applicable advertising cookies are used.

Children

Hum Runner is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not ask players to create an account or provide personal details.

Third-party links

This site may link to third-party websites, including analytics and Google information pages. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the site or its services change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.
